Plain-language privacy

Privacy policy

This site was built to collect as little as possible. Here is exactly what it does and does not do with information about you, in ordinary language.

Updated October 2026 4 minute read Denver, Colorado

Effective date: 12 January 2026. Last reviewed: 6 October 2026. This policy applies to bestseocompanydenverco.com, published by Eye To Ad Media of 1001 Bannock St #660, Denver, Colorado 80204.

The short version

  • No analytics, no advertising pixels, no cookies set by us, no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • The scorecard tool saves your scores in your own browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to us.
  • The contact form on the About page sends your message to our inbox through a third-party form service. That is the only way this site ever receives personal information.
  • The site is hosted on a static hosting service, which keeps standard server logs.

What we collect, and what we do not

Browsing this site sends us nothing. There is no tracking script on any page, we do not set cookies, and we do not load fonts, icons, scripts or images from any domain other than this one. If you check the network activity in your browser's developer tools, you should see requests only to bestseocompanydenverco.com. If you ever see otherwise, please tell us through the contact form; it would be a mistake.

We do not collect your IP address, device information or browsing history ourselves. The hosting provider that serves the files may keep standard access logs (request time, path, IP address, browser string) for security and operational reasons under its own policy. We do not request or analyze those logs.

The scorecard tool

The interactive scorecard stores the agency names, scores and notes you enter in your browser's local storage, under a single key, on the device you are using. That data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it and send it somewhere. It persists until you clear it with the reset button, clear your browser's site data, or use a private window that discards it when closed. We have no way to see it, recover it, or delete it for you.

If you share a computer, be aware that anyone using the same browser profile can see what you entered. Use the reset button when you are done if that matters to you.

The contact form

The form on the About page is processed by formsubmit.co, a third-party service that forwards submissions by email. When you submit the form, the fields you filled in (name, email address, topic and message) are transmitted to that service and delivered to info (at) eyetoad.com. The service's own privacy policy governs its handling of the submission in transit. We do not store form submissions on this site.

We use what you send to reply to you. If you asked about hiring Eye To Ad Media, we will treat the message as a sales enquiry and may follow up by email or phone; you can tell us to stop at any time. We do not add contact form senders to marketing lists, and we do not sell or rent contact information to anyone.

The form includes a hidden field designed to catch automated submissions. A human filling in the visible fields will never see or trigger it.

Links to other sites

A small number of pages link to eyetoad.com, the publisher's own site, which has its own privacy policy. We do not link to any site that pays us to do so. We are not responsible for the practices of sites we link to, including Google's documentation pages that we refer to by description.

Children

This site is written for business owners and managers and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and since the only collection point is the contact form, we would delete any such submission on becoming aware of it.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold almost nothing, your rights are simple to exercise. If you have contacted us through the form and want your message and our reply deleted from our email, write to the same address and ask; we will confirm when it is done. If you are a Colorado resident, the Colorado Privacy Act gives you rights of access, correction, deletion and opt-out in relation to businesses that process personal data at scale; this site does not meet those thresholds, but we will honour the same requests as a matter of practice.

Residents of other jurisdictions with privacy laws (for example, the EU, UK or California) may make the same requests by the same route. We will respond within 30 days.

Hosting

The site is a set of static files served from a hosting platform's edge network. We have not added any server-side code, redirects that record visits, or edge functions. The platform may apply its own caching and logging under its own terms, which we have no ability to change.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Because there is no database, no login and no server-side code, there is nothing on this site to breach beyond the public pages themselves. Email, which is where contact-form messages end up, is only as secure as email generally is; please do not send passwords, financial details or anything confidential through the form.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site works in a way that affects what is collected, for example by adding analytics, we will update this page, change the review date at the top, and note the change in the "updated" line of the site's About page. We do not expect to add tracking; part of the point of this site is to show that a useful marketing publication can run without it.

Write to Eye To Ad Media, 1001 Bannock St #660, Denver, CO 80204, email info (at) eyetoad.com, or call 1-800-481-8638. Put "privacy" in the subject line and it will reach the right person faster.
